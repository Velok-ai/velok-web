# VELOK painted direction

The visual sequence is intended for French professional-services leaders and teams who need to see practical work and human guidance quickly. Illustrated people and scenes are illustrative, not customer testimonials.

## Shared visual language

- Large gouache scenes use blue, mineral teal and warm porcelain, with deliberate edge crops and quiet gallery captions.
- Nine original painted cutouts sit on white: a real work folder, guided practice, an AI-assisted document, a repeatable practice and a document review.
- The first-use story runs from a real example to an assisted AI attempt to a reviewed practice the team can reuse. Actual buyer comprehension still requires user testing.
- Primary actions share a dark teal fill; secondary actions use a teal outline. Keyboard focus remains visibly blue.
- Navigation names the two primary offers directly: Ateliers and AI Sherpa. The inbox audit remains secondary.

## Assets

Web images are versioned with the website in `public/brand/paintings/` and `public/brand/cutouts/`. All twelve delivery files are WebP. Source PNGs and generation prompts are working archives, not runtime dependencies or hotlinked Drive files.

## Motion and accessibility

Off-screen sections enter once with a 10 px movement and soft fade. Initial content stays available without JavaScript. Reduced-motion preferences disable reveals, transitions and smooth scrolling. Button arrows move slightly on hover. No looping scenes or parallax.

The contact form focuses and scrolls to the next step heading; the mobile menu closes after navigation. A keyboard skip link leads to the main content.

## Positioning guardrails

No displayed prices, invented customer outcomes or guaranteed ROI. Existing tools precede custom development. Implementation is separately scoped. Security and human control support the offer.

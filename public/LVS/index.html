02 / A target to validate after 60 days

Build toward a measurable
monthly pipeline.

Our proposed month-three target is below. The first 60 days establish the sources, workflow and early evidence needed to confirm or revise it.

Planning scenarios · not a forecast or lead guarantee
12Qualified enquiries / monthRelevant declared need, preliminary fit and an agreed next step.
8Held relevant meetingsConversations your team can prepare for and progress.
4Professional assessmentsCases progressing to the appropriate human review.

“Qualified” means commercial meeting fit against LVS-approved criteria, based on declared circumstances. It does not mean confirmed wealth, tax eligibility or investment suitability.

How would we get to those numbers?
  • 6 active introducers × 2 introductions = 12 enquiries.
  • 100 relevant landing-page visits × 8% enquiry rate = 8 enquiries.
  • 20 total enquiries × 60% commercial fit = 12 qualified enquiries.
  • Two-thirds reach a held meeting; half progress to assessment.

Every rate is an assumption. Introducers are not yet secured and traffic is not established. LVS must have capacity for eight meetings and four assessments per month. Review actual source yield, quality, cost and timing at day 60; revise the target if the evidence does not support it. Closed clients and tax outcomes are not forecast.
